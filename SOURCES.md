# Sources and editorial limits

## Core framework

Donald Miller, *Building a StoryBrand* and *Building a StoryBrand 2.0*. The seven-element customer-story framework is attributed to Miller and StoryBrand. See [StoryBrand](https://storybrand.com/) for the official organization and its materials.

## Supporting teaching

Each of the [ten lessons](.agents/skills/storybrand-messaging/references/lessons.md) links to the public video associated with that topic in the source notes. Titles may be shortened for readability. Links are attribution, not endorsements or scientific substantiation.

## How this kit was prepared

This kit was written as new, concise summaries after reviewing an existing internal StoryBrand skill and ten Donald Miller lesson notes. None of the internal files, transcripts, PDFs, client examples, or private source paths are redistributed. The summaries intentionally soften unsupported absolutes and separate practical exercises from evidence.

## What this kit does not claim

- No official affiliation, endorsement, or certification.
- No guaranteed conversion, revenue, recall, or sales-cycle improvement.
- No neurological claims or numerical loss-aversion multipliers.
- No validated cognitive-load scoring scale, universal repetition count, or optimal video length.
- No permission to reuse third-party testimonials, footage, logos, or book text.

Use original business facts, obtain permission for customer stories, and review every factual claim before publishing. Consult official materials for the complete method; this kit is an independent working aid.
